Our direction

A shared commitment to better health education.

The institute profile sets out an ambition for learners and educators to shape meaningful, student-centred education together.

Our Vision

Build a learning community where educators and learners co-create student-centred, evidence-based, innovative and technology-rich programs that strengthen nursing, midwifery and allied health education and support excellence in care for the nation.

Our Mission

Prepare nursing, midwifery and allied health graduates to meet healthcare needs through the nursing process, critical thinking, evidence-based care, clinical reasoning and innovation. Help learners develop compassion, integrity, clinical proficiency, therapeutic communication and ethical conduct.
